# Synthesis | Personas and pains discovery

**Decision this informs:** Which personas exist within professionals who use news/information to inform stakeholders' opinions, policies, or actions — and where do their pains converge or diverge? Feeds Tempo product direction and persona confidence. Per the [evidence → action trigger table](guide--personas-and-pains-discovery.md): current state = **+1 new independent participant** → still 🟠 → **log only; no product action**.

**Research question:** Do professionals whose core work involves consuming and analyzing news/information — in order to inform internal and/or external stakeholders' opinions, policies, and/or actions — share a similar pain shape across the consume → analyze → act loop, regardless of team size or tooling? Where do pains diverge by persona?

**Hypothesis (wave 2 / active):** Professionals who use news/information to inform stakeholders share similar pains in how they consume, analyze, and act on it — regardless of team size or tools. Confirmed → build shared solutions across personas. Disconfirmed → pains cluster by persona/role with no meaningful cross-persona overlap.

**Falsifiability test:** Code each participant's top-3 independently. **Confirmed** if ≥2 recurring themes across ≥3 distinct personas/roles. **Disconfirmed** if pains cluster entirely by persona with no cross-persona thematic overlap.

**Scope:** Rolling synthesis. Current evidence: wave 1 (Mercedes Osma, 2 sessions, Apr 16–17 2026) + wave 2 session 1 (Vanessa Aguirre, 11 Sep 26). **N=2 participants, 3 sessions.**

**Active guide:** [Interview guide — personas and pains discovery](guide--personas-and-pains-discovery.md)

## Executive summary

Lead with limits: at **N=2** the wave-2 shared-pain hypothesis is **⚠️ Inconclusive**. The falsifiability bar needs ≥3 distinct personas with ≥2 recurring themes; we have two adjacent embassy roles and one strong recurring theme candidate (consume/monitor load as #1), not confirmation.

**Disconfirming / thin [H-] first:** pain *mechanisms* diverge. Mercedes’s #1 is interruptive self-checking **while drafting** crisis statements. Vanessa’s #1 is morning **volume/time-to-process** (~3–4 hours) to select, summarize, sentiment-tag, and report to Lima — she did not describe concurrent drafting interruption, and when asked for two more pains she did not produce them. Team shape also differs: Mercedes day-to-day solo vs Vanessa press cell (self + 2 interns) inside a larger political department.

**Confirming [H+] (directional only):** both name information-landscape consumption/analysis as the dominant burden and first thing to improve; both rely on X + Google without a dedicated embassy monitoring platform; both improvise AI (copy-paste / conversational) under constraints; both produce stakeholder-facing outputs from that loop (public/reactive statements vs Lima impact briefs).

**Persona call:** keep p1 (`working`, solo) on Mercedes evidence; add **p2 hypothesized** for Vanessa’s small-team embassy press/political role — do not stretch “solo” to cover her. Confidence remains 🟠; **no product action**.
