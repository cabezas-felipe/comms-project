## Beliefs

**Confirmed (evidence so far — still small-N; directional only):**

- **Consume/monitor load ranks #1** for both Mercedes (p1) and Vanessa (p2 hyp.) — shared *priority label*, not yet a shared *mechanism* (sessions 1–3).
- **Wave 1 (p1 only):** core pain is interruptive scanning while writing; calm vs fast modes; speed+completeness for crisis statements; laptop/split-screen preference (sessions 1–2).
- **Wave 2 (p2 only so far):** morning volume (~3–4h); X→Google choreography; sentiment + narrative axes + AP/Reuters weight; output = concise Lima brief; AI copy-paste under no-sensitive-upload policy (session 3).

**Assumed (needs validation):**

- “Solo” is default day-to-day for p1; **crisis mode** may **share monitoring** across colleagues (session 1).
- p2 is a distinct persona vs a solo/small-team variant of one embassy-bilateral type — **hypothesized**, not validated.
- Tacit triage rules can be partially externalized into product **if** trust tradeoffs are tested with real cases.

**Unknown / not yet evidenced:**

- Prevalence beyond two embassy-adjacent operators; non-embassy roles entirely missing.
- Replication of interruption-while-drafting outside Mercedes.
- Whether digest / “what changed” / ranked feed reduces interruption without increasing misses (evaluative — not licensed yet).
- Broader regulatory/procurement reality beyond one embassy’s AI rule.
